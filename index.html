About This Journal
This pain management journal is based on the Daily Pain Tracker Diary created by Barbara Ford-Hammond (BFH) as part of her Pain Management Resource Pack.

It helps you track your daily pain, triggers, coping mechanisms, and well-being — privately, on your own device. Nothing here is intended to replace medical help or treatment.
How To Use
Fill in each day's entry — even partial notes are valuable. Your data saves automatically as you type.

History shows all past entries. Stats reveals patterns over time — when pain is highest, energy trends, and mood patterns. These can be useful to share with your doctor or care team.
Energy & Spoons
The energy section uses Spoon Theory. Each of the 10 boxes represents 10% of your overall energy. Tap to show how much has been used today. Over time, this reveals which activities or pain levels cost the most energy.
Privacy
All data is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere. To keep your data safe, avoid clearing your browser storage. If you reinstall or switch devices, your history will not transfer automatically.
